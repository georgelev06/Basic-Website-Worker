Our story

Born in a docking bay. Built for the whole galaxy.

In 2471, a small team of engineers, designers and interspecies comfort specialists opened a workshop on the edge of the Vega trade lanes. They believed that every vessel — no matter how old, unusual or far-travelled — deserved a second life.

Today, Starhaven brings together craftspeople from across twelve systems. We combine careful engineering with thoughtful interior design to make ships feel welcoming, personal and ready for the next chapter.
